Getting Started

Add your first feeds and learn the basics.

First Launch

When you first open Broadsheet Reader, you'll see a walkthrough introducing the key features. You can skip it or revisit it anytime from Help > Welcome to Broadsheet Reader.

After the walkthrough, you'll be offered a set of curated starter feeds covering AI, tech, and research. Select the ones you're interested in and they'll be added automatically.

Adding a Feed

Press ⌘Shift+N or click the + button in the toolbar and choose Add Feed. Paste in a website URL or a direct RSS/Atom feed URL — Broadsheet Reader will automatically discover the feed.

Tip: You don't need to find the exact RSS URL. Just paste the website address (like example.com) and Broadsheet Reader will find the feed for you.

The Three-Column Layout

Broadsheet Reader uses a three-column layout:

  • Sidebar (left) — Navigate between Today, All Items, Bookmarks, Search, and your feed groups
  • Article List (center) — Browse articles with unread indicators and summaries
  • Article Detail (right) — Read the full article content

Navigating

Click any section in the sidebar to view its articles. Use ⌘1 through ⌘3 to jump to Today, All Items, or Bookmarks. Press ⌘F to search.

Move between articles with ⌘↓ and ⌘↑, or use the arrow keys when the article list has focus.
