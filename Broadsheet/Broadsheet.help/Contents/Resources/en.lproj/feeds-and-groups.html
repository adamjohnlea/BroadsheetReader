Feeds & Groups

Organize, refresh, and manage your subscriptions.

Adding Feeds

Press ⌘Shift+N or use File > Add Feed. Enter a website URL or feed URL. You can optionally assign the feed to a group when adding it.

Creating Groups

Use File > New Group to create a feed group. Groups appear as collapsible sections in the sidebar, each showing an unread count badge.

Organizing Feeds

  • Move to a group — Right-click a feed and choose Move to Group
  • Reorder — Drag and drop feeds and groups in the sidebar to change their order
  • Collapse/expand — Click the disclosure triangle next to a group name

Refreshing Feeds

Press ⌘R or click the refresh button in the toolbar to refresh all feeds. You can also right-click an individual feed and choose Refresh.

Broadsheet Reader can refresh feeds automatically in the background. Configure the interval in Settings > Auto-Refresh.

Feed Errors

If a feed fails to refresh, a warning icon appears next to it in the sidebar. Hover over the icon to see the error message. Common causes include the feed URL changing or the server being temporarily unavailable.

Deleting Feeds

Right-click a feed and choose Delete Feed. This removes the feed and all its articles. This action requires confirmation.

Deleting a group moves its feeds to the ungrouped section — the feeds themselves are not deleted.
