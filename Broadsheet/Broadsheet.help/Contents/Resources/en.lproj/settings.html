Settings

Refresh intervals, article retention, and preferences.

Open Settings with ⌘, or from the app menu.

Reading

Mark articles as read when opened

When enabled (the default), selecting an article in the list automatically marks it as read. Turn this off if you prefer to mark articles read manually.

Auto-Refresh

Refresh interval

Choose how often Broadsheet Reader automatically fetches new articles in the background:

  • Manual only — feeds are only refreshed when you press ⌘R
  • Every 15 minutes
  • Every 30 minutes (default)
  • Every 60 minutes

The app also refreshes all feeds once when it launches.

Article Retention

How long to keep articles

Controls how long old articles remain visible:

  • 7 days — only the last week of articles
  • 30 days (default) — the last month
  • 90 days — the last three months
  • Forever — never hide old articles
Note: Retention only affects visibility — articles aren't permanently deleted. Bookmarked articles are always visible regardless of the retention setting.

iCloud Sync

Shows whether iCloud sync is currently active. This is read-only — sync is enabled automatically when iCloud is available. See iCloud Sync for troubleshooting.
