Bookmarks & Notes

Save articles and add your own annotations.

Bookmarking an Article

Click the bookmark button in the article toolbar, or right-click an article in the list and choose Bookmark. Bookmarked articles show a green bookmark icon in the list.

Adding Notes

When an article is bookmarked, a note editor appears below the article header. Type your thoughts, highlights, or reminders — notes save automatically as you type.

Note snippets are also visible in the article list, so you can see your annotations at a glance.

Viewing Bookmarks

Click Bookmarks in the sidebar or press ⌘3 to see all your bookmarked articles in one place. The bookmarks view always shows all bookmarks regardless of the read/unread filter.

Bookmarks and Retention

Important: Bookmarked articles are never automatically deleted by the retention policy. Even if you set retention to 7 days, your bookmarks are preserved indefinitely.

Removing a Bookmark

Click the bookmark button again in the toolbar, or right-click and choose Remove Bookmark. The article and any associated note will be unbookmarked.
