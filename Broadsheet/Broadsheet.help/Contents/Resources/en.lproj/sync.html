iCloud Sync

Keep your feeds and read state in sync across devices.

What Syncs

When iCloud sync is active, the following data syncs automatically across all your devices signed into the same Apple Account:

  • Feeds and feed groups
  • Articles and their read/unread state
  • Bookmarks and notes
  • Group collapse state and sort order

Checking Sync Status

Open Settings (⌘,) and look at the iCloud Sync section. A green checkmark indicates sync is active. An "X" means the app is using local-only storage.

Troubleshooting

Sync is not active

  • Make sure you're signed into iCloud in System Settings > Apple Account
  • Check that iCloud Drive is enabled
  • Ensure Broadsheet Reader has permission to use iCloud (System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Apps Using iCloud)

Changes are slow to appear

CloudKit sync is not instant — changes typically propagate within a few minutes. Large initial syncs may take longer.

Local fallback

If iCloud is unavailable, Broadsheet Reader automatically falls back to local-only storage. Your data is safe — it's stored on your Mac. Sync will resume when iCloud becomes available again.
