Search

Find articles across all your feeds.

How to Search

Click Search in the sidebar or press ⌘F. Type your query in the search bar at the top of the window. Results appear instantly as you type.

What's Searched

Broadsheet Reader searches across:

  • Article titles
  • Article summaries
  • Full article content
  • Feed names

Search is case-insensitive — searching for "apple" will match "Apple", "APPLE", etc.

Search Scope

Search looks through all visible articles, respecting your current retention settings. Bookmarked articles are always included in search results regardless of age.
