Reading Articles

Navigate, filter, and share your articles.

Unread Indicators

Unread articles are marked with a green dot on the left side of the article list. Article titles appear in bold when unread and regular weight when read.

By default, articles are automatically marked as read when you select them. You can change this in Settings > Reading.

Filtering

Use the All / Unread toggle at the top of the article list to switch between viewing all articles or only unread ones. This filter is remembered separately for each section (Today, All Items, individual feeds, etc.).

Marking Articles

  • Mark as read/unread — Click the envelope button in the toolbar, or right-click an article and choose the option
  • Mark all read — Click Mark All Read in the filter bar to mark every visible article as read

Navigating Articles

  • ⌘↓ — Next article
  • ⌘↑ — Previous article
  • Arrow keys also work when the article list has focus

Sharing

Click the share button in the article toolbar to share via AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Notes, or any other sharing extension installed on your Mac.

Opening in Browser

Click the Safari button in the article toolbar to open the full article in your default web browser.

Deleting Articles

Right-click an article and choose Delete Article. Deleted articles are hidden permanently — they won't reappear even if the feed is refreshed.
